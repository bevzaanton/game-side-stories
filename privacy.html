Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The "Game Side Stories" upload tool is a private script used only by the owner of the Game Side Stories YouTube channels to upload their own videos, manage playlists and read their own channel statistics through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

What data is accessed

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool receives an OAuth token for the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses it to upload videos, update video details and playlists, and read view statistics of those videos.

How data is stored

The token and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Other users

The tool is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
